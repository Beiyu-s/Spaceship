ABOUT ME

I am a musician with a background in both jazz and classical music. My journey began with classical voice training at Hunan Institute of Science and Technology, where I earned my first Bachelor's degree in Music Education. Later, I pursued my passion for jazz at California State University Northridge, earning a second Bachelor's degree in Jazz Studies.

My musical experiences span across various ensembles, including jazz bands, symphony orchestras, and vocal ensembles. I've had the privilege of studying with renowned musicians like Erin Bentlage, Lucy Nargizyan, and Mary Durst, who have helped shape my unique sound and approach to music.

Beyond performance, I am passionate about music education and have taught piano, voice, guitar, and music theory to students of all ages. I believe in the transformative power of music and strive to inspire that same passion in my students.
